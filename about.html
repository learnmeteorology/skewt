About this site

Skew-T Guide is a free introduction to reading a Skew-T log-P diagram. It is written for beginners, so no experience is needed.

What you can do

  • LearnTake short interactive lessons, or build a chart one layer at a time in the reference.
  • ExploreLook up lines, change the temperature and dewpoint, and see how a lifted parcel responds.
  • PracticeIdentify chart features and plot data, with hints.
  • TestCheck what you know. It is a self-check and is not suitable for graded exams.

Please keep in mind

Not for forecasting or safety decisions. For real weather warnings, use your official weather service.

Examples
The example soundings are made up for teaching. They are not observations.
What is calculated
The parcel results in Explore, Practice and Test use a parcel starting at the surface. A few Learn examples, such as wet-bulb temperature and the Showalter Index, start at 850 mb. There is no hodograph.
Moisture correction
To stay simple, Learn, Practice and Test compare actual temperatures. The Full chart in Explore also allows for moisture (virtual temperature), so its values differ.
Full chart rules
It counts each warm layer of more than 1 J/kg, even a very small one near the ground, and its CIN includes colder layers up to the highest EL. This can differ from textbooks and other software.

How the numbers were checked

The site's calculations were compared with MetPy 1.7.1, an open-source weather library, using the three examples and warmer and cooler versions of them.

ValueCompared with MetPy
LCL (lifting condensation level)Agreed within 1 mb
EL (equilibrium level)Agreed within 3 mb
CAPE, Full chartThe site was up to about 9% higher
CIN, Full chartAgreed within 15 J/kg
LFC (level of free convection)Can differ more when the site counts a warm layer starting at the ground

This is not a guarantee that every value or sentence is correct.
